About

Singapore

I build tools for real work, then check the numbers to see whether they helped.

I’m Evan Yap, an Engineering Systems and Design student at the Singapore University of Technology and Design. Most of what I make sits where data, automation and AI meet: agents that do the research, models that check the assumptions, and interfaces that make the result easy to act on.

Right now I’m an AI business consultant intern at Univers, working with aviation, transportation and logistics clients on decarbonisation and energy management.

Away from the keyboard I captain the SUTD men’s floorball team, train for HYROX and powerlifting, play the guitar, drink a lot of coffee, and serve in my church’s worship ministry.
