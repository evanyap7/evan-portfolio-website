## 11. Deviations from the spec (plan, "flag to Evan")

1. Project list pattern. Projects use one disclosure pattern (a button with aria-expanded controlling a region) at every size, not ARIA tabs on desktop. This avoids switching roles at a breakpoint, which screen readers handle badly. Arrow keys, Home and End still move between rows, and on wide screens they also open the row.
2. Reduced-motion note. It is a visible line in the cartoon's caption ("still: Reduce Motion is on"), shown only when Reduce Motion is on, not screen-reader-only text. The people who mistake stillness for a fault are sighted visitors like Evan on his iPhone.
3. No safe-area padding. The page does not set viewport-fit=cover, so Safari keeps content clear of notches itself and no env(safe-area-inset-*) padding is needed.
4. Menu breakpoint. The menu replaces the bar's navigation below 1024px, so tablets get it too, not only phones.
5. Sheet-fit sizes. The fit is checked from 1280px wide, as the spec's Testing section says. Between 1024 and 1279 the desktop layout applies and sheets may grow.
6. Sheet notes. The sheet heads gain short mono notes ("Singapore", "2023 – now", "Five projects · 2026", "Updated Oct 2026").
7. Case study wording. Its note "Pink marks the only step that was ever a model" becomes "Blue marks…", because the colour changes.
8. Phone headline size. The Intro headline is 36–41px on phones (the spec said 44px). In `css/sheets.css`.
9. Phone underline. On phones the "check they work." underline is a static text underline, not the drawn stroke. In `css/sheets.css`.
10. Copy button position. On phones 375px wide and up, the Copy button sits beside the email address instead of on its own line. In `css/sheets.css`.
11. Small body text. Experience points and toolkit values are 14px (the spec gave body text as 15–18px). In `css/sheets.css` and `css/layout.css`.
12. Menu focus. Choosing a menu link leaves focus at the chosen sheet instead of returning it to the Menu button. In `js/menu.js`.
13. Diagram track contrast. The meter and gauge tracks in the diagrams are about 1.6:1 against white (the spec asked for 3:1 or more on figure strokes). The values are also shown as text. In `css/figures.css`.
14. One screen only on landscape. One-screen sheets apply only to landscape screens at least 1024 × 650. Portrait tablets, such as an iPad Pro held upright, get sheets that grow with their content. In `css/layout.css`.

## 12. Later decisions made while building

- Short-laptop layout. On screens 760px tall or less, sheet titles are 32px and spacing is tighter, so each sheet still fits one screen.
- The Toolkit sheet uses three columns (Toolkit, Recognition, Education with Certifications).
- On laptops 1280px wide or more and 799px tall or less, a project's diagram sits beside its summary instead of above it, so the text stays legible.
- Diagram text is larger than the plan first specified, so notes stay at 9px or more.
- While the menu is open, the page behind it is inert: Tab and screen readers stay inside the menu, and focus stays in it.
- The bar clock stays on one line and no longer wraps.
- The Fig. 4.1 homepage preview shows the whole screenshot, letterboxed in its frame on the figure's dot grid at every size, and never cropped. On large desktops it fills the frame's height, is about half its width, and sits centred with empty grid either side.
